\begin{figure*}[t]

    \caption{Trace-driven discrete-event simulation of experience upload over a shared uplink, driven by
    the measured raw-episode and distilled-experience payload sizes of the 24{,}000 paired events.
    (a) P95 upload completion time versus the number of clients, with one upload per second per client
    and a shared uplink of 1~Mbps.
    (b) P95 completion-time reduction, $(1-\mathrm{P95}_{\mathrm{exp}}/\mathrm{P95}_{\mathrm{raw}})$,
    versus the offered load generated by raw uploads at 50 clients, with the 77.5\% payload reduction shown
    as a reference. Every event carries both representations and the two share identical arrivals,
    client assignment and link conditions, so only the payload differs; the queue is a single FIFO
    server with service time $8D_i/R$ (no protocol overhead, $H=0$). Markers are means over 30 seeds and
    bars show one standard deviation across seeds. Completion time includes queueing and transmission
    but excludes inference, distillation and backend condensation.}
    \label{fig:upload_time}
